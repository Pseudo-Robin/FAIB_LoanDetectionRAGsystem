# Illustrative Lending Policy

> DEMO DOCUMENT ONLY. This fictional policy exists to demonstrate retrieval and citation. It is not an actual bank policy, legal advice, or a compliance-approved lending standard.

### P01 - Scope and human review
This sample policy describes an initial screening process for an unsecured personal loan. A model score is not a lending decision. A trained reviewer must consider the complete application, verify the information, and document the final decision. Applicants must receive clear reasons and any notices required by applicable law.

### P02 - Repayment capacity
Reviewers should assess whether verified, stable income can support the requested payment after ordinary monthly expenses and existing debt obligations. For this demonstration only, a proposed total monthly debt-to-income ratio above 40% requires manual review; it is not an automatic decline rule. Calculate the ratio using monthly debt obligations divided by verified gross monthly income. If income or obligations cannot be verified, request documentation rather than assuming a value.

### P03 - Credit history
Review the applicant's documented repayment history, current delinquencies, and credit file for accuracy. For this demonstration, a score below 600 triggers manual review, while scores from 600 through 659 require additional review of repayment capacity. These illustrative bands are not approval thresholds. Consider relevant context and allow applicants to correct inaccurate information.

### P04 - Employment and income stability
Verify income source, amount, and continuity using appropriate documentation. Employment status alone is not a substitute for assessing repayment capacity. For this demonstration, less than 12 months in the current income source may prompt a request for additional evidence of stable income; it must not by itself determine the outcome.

### P05 - Loan terms and disclosures
The applicant must be able to review the requested principal, annual percentage rate, fees, repayment schedule, and term before acceptance. For this demonstration, unsecured personal-loan terms are between 12 and 84 months. Any exception requires documented human review. Do not present an estimated rate or payment as a firm offer.

### P06 - Fair treatment and prohibited inputs
Treat applicants consistently and evaluate only verified, relevant information. Do not use protected characteristics, including gender, as a basis for a lending decision. Review models and data for disparate impact, proxy variables, data leakage, and accuracy before any operational use. The supplied demo model expects a Gender feature; that is a material fairness and compliance concern and makes its predictions unsuitable for real lending.

### P07 - Missing information, exceptions, and adverse action
When information is missing or inconsistent, pause screening and request clarification or refer the application to a human reviewer. Record the evidence and reasons for exceptions. A model label alone is not a sufficient explanation for an adverse decision. Provide any specific reasons and applicant notices required by applicable law.
